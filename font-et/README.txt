License notes
Font.et is an open Ethiopic type archive. Many families are SIL Open Font License
or GPL. About half have no license listed in the catalog. The site asks you to
review the license bundled with each font before commercial use.

A few unlabeled or mislabeled families may still be proprietary vendor fonts
(for example Google Sans, Nyala, Kefa). Check those before shipping a product.

manifest.json lists every family, file, and skipped item.
